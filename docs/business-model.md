# Business Model: Community Library and Archive

## Classification
- Repository: `cloud-itonami-9101`
- ISIC Rev.5: `9101` — library and archive operations — cataloging, lending, preservation and exhibition
- Social impact: knowledge-access cultural-preservation inclusion

## Customer
- community libraries, archives and schools leaving closed ILS SaaS

## Offer
- catalog and lending, preservation workflows, exhibition records, access and audit

## Revenue
- setup fee per library, monthly operations subscription, preservation and integration services

## Trust Controls
- lending outside policy is blocked; preservation is auditable; patron data stays outside Git
- a robot action the governor refuses is never dispatched to hardware
- every dispatch, hold, approval and disclosure path is auditable
- sensitive operating and personal data stays outside Git
